What we built

VITALIS currently has nine main tabs. The information available depends on whether someone signs in as a crew member or as a crew manager.

Crew Member Account

A crew member can use the app for their own information and cannot access the full crew-management view.

  • Home: Personal status, fatigue information, and recommendations.
  • Schedule: View the schedule and mission events relevant to planning.
  • Sleep Plan: Generate and save a personalized sleep plan.
  • Crew: Opens a personal profile view rather than the full crew manager.
  • Food: Track personal food and caffeine information.
  • Shift: View the assigned work shift.
  • Check-In: Record sleep, alertness, fatigue, comments, and how effective the previous recommendation was.
  • History: Review personal check-ins, feedback, and saved sleep recommendations.
  • Settings: Change app appearance, font options, and account settings.

Crew Manager Account

A manager has access to broader crew information and can select crew members in supported screens.

  • Home: View the overall crew dashboard.
  • Schedule: Review mission events that may affect sleep opportunities.
  • Sleep Plan: Select a crew member and generate their plan using available sleep and fatigue information.
  • Crew: View and manage crew profiles.
  • Food: Select crew members and review their food and caffeine tracking.
  • Shift: Review and manage crew shift assignments.
  • Check-In: Select a crew member and save a check-in and recommendation feedback.
  • History: Review crew members’ check-ins, feedback, and saved recommendations.
  • Settings: Change app appearance, font options, and account settings.

Authentication

Email sign-in and verification identify the account and its assigned role.

Local Data

The prototype stores operational app data in a local SQLite database, including sleep logs, mission events, shifts, food records, feedback, and recommendations.

Recommendation Feedback

Check-ins can be linked to the recommendation being rated, allowing the system to consider past effectiveness when planning.

The role-based screens are part of the current prototype. Full secure multi-device data synchronization and public deployment are future work, not completed features.
