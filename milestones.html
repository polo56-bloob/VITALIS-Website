AUGUST 2026

Project beginning

We brainstormed solutions for astronaut sleep scheduling and explored hardware, biological sleep timing, health tracking, and recommendation systems.

First working prototype

We built the first software prototype and tested its sleep recommendations.

SEPTEMBER–OCTOBER 2026 • MILESTONE 2

Personalized scheduling system

We added fatigue scoring, shifts, mission events, crew management, food/caffeine tracking, check-ins, recommendations, feedback, authentication, and history.
