  9. National Aeronautics and Space Administration. (n.d.). Risk of inadequate food and nutrition. https://www.nasa.gov/reference/risk-of-inadequate-food-and-nutrition/

    Supported our understanding of why nutrition matters for crew health and performance.

  10. National Aeronautics and Space Administration. (n.d.). Space food systems. https://www.nasa.gov/directorates/esdmd/hhp/space-food-systems/

    Provided background on planning and managing food for spaceflight, informing our prototype food and inventory ideas.

  11. National Aeronautics and Space Administration. (n.d.). JSC food and nutrition. https://www.nasa.gov/reference/jsc-food-nutrition/

    Helped ground our food and nutrition research in NASA’s crew-health context.

  12. National Aeronautics and Space Administration. (n.d.). Crew systems. https://www.nasa.gov/reference/crew-systems/

    Provided broader context for the systems and crew needs that a spaceflight tool must consider.

  13. National Aeronautics and Space Administration. (n.d.). Habitability functions, Volume 2. https://www.nasa.gov/reference/7-0-habit-ability-functions-vol-2/

    Helped us consider habitability and crew-support requirements when framing the project.

  14. National Aeronautics and Space Administration. (n.d.). Artemis II: What’s on the menu? https://www.nasa.gov/missions/artemis/artemis-2/artemis-ii-whats-on-the-menu/

    Provided an example of food planned for a real NASA mission and helped inform our NASA-inspired prototype food list.

  15. National Aeronautics and Space Administration. (n.d.). Space nutrition. https://science.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Space_Nutrition_Book.pdf

    Provided additional background on nutrition during spaceflight. Our in-app food values remain prototype estimates, not official NASA nutritional data.
