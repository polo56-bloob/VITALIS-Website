References

References are listed in an APA-style format. Each description explains how the source supported our research or prototype development.

  1. National Aeronautics and Space Administration. (n.d.). Food in space. https://www.nasa.gov/ochmo/food-in-space/

    Helped us understand food considerations in space and informed the context for the food-tracking feature.

  2. National Aeronautics and Space Administration. (n.d.). Risk of inadequate food and nutrition. https://www.nasa.gov/reference/risk-of-inadequate-food-and-nutrition/

    Supported our understanding of why nutrition matters for crew health and performance.

  3. National Aeronautics and Space Administration. (n.d.). Space food systems. https://www.nasa.gov/directorates/esdmd/hhp/space-food-systems/

    Provided background on planning and managing food for spaceflight, informing our prototype food and inventory ideas.

  4. National Aeronautics and Space Administration. (n.d.). JSC food and nutrition. https://www.nasa.gov/reference/jsc-food-nutrition/

    Helped ground our food and nutrition research in NASA’s crew-health context.

  5. National Aeronautics and Space Administration. (n.d.). Crew systems. https://www.nasa.gov/reference/crew-systems/

    Provided broader context for the systems and crew needs that a spaceflight tool must consider.

  6. National Aeronautics and Space Administration. (n.d.). Habitability functions, Volume 2. https://www.nasa.gov/reference/7-0-habit-ability-functions-vol-2/

    Helped us consider habitability and crew-support requirements when framing the project.

  7. National Aeronautics and Space Administration. (n.d.). Artemis II: What’s on the menu? https://www.nasa.gov/missions/artemis/artemis-2/artemis-ii-whats-on-the-menu/

    Provided an example of food planned for a real NASA mission and helped inform our NASA-inspired prototype food list.

  8. National Aeronautics and Space Administration. (n.d.). Space nutrition. https://science.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Space_Nutrition_Book.pdf

    Provided additional background on nutrition during spaceflight. Our in-app food values remain prototype estimates, not official NASA nutritional data.

  9. Flutter. (n.d.). Flutter documentation. https://docs.flutter.dev/

    Used as a reference while building the app interface, navigation, screens, and desktop prototype.

  10. Dart. (n.d.). Dart documentation. https://dart.dev/

    Supported our coding work and helped us understand the language used to build VITALIS.

  11. Supabase. (n.d.). Supabase documentation. https://supabase.com/docs

    Used while implementing email authentication and user profiles for account roles.

  12. FlutterBluePlus contributors. (n.d.). FlutterBluePlus. pub.dev. https://pub.dev/packages/flutter_blue_plus

    Helped us research possible Bluetooth connectivity for future wearable or sensor integration.

  13. sqflite_common_ffi contributors. (n.d.). sqflite_common_ffi. pub.dev. https://pub.dev/packages/sqflite_common_ffi

    Supported our local SQLite database setup for storing prototype information on a desktop computer.

  14. Dart packages. (n.d.). path. pub.dev. https://pub.dev/packages/path

    Provided utilities for working with file and database paths in the local app.
