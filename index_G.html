01 • Project Overview

NASA HUNCH + Our Work

VITALIS is our student-developed prototype for the NASA HUNCH SFT 2026–27 Lunar Sleep-Shift Scheduling Tool. NASA HUNCH challenged us to develop an onboard, offline system that can help astronauts manage sleep and work-shift changes while reducing fatigue during missions. The concept is designed to provide personalized sleep and wake recommendations, naps, caffeine and sleep-aid timing, fatigue monitoring, and feedback while still allowing mission operations to override recommendations when necessary.

Our team turned that challenge into VITALIS, a software-based prototype focused on making the system practical and accessible on mission computers or tablets. We researched astronaut sleep, circadian rhythms, fatigue, nutrition, caffeine, and wearable technology, then moved from our early hardware/wearable concept toward a software system. We built the prototype with Flutter and Dart and developed features including crew profiles, authentication, sleep planning, fatigue scoring, shift scheduling, mission events, food and caffeine tracking, check-ins, saved recommendations, feedback, and history. We continue to test the system with different inputs and refine the scheduling logic so that recommendations can better respond to each crew member and the demands of mission operations.

02 • The Problem

Why VITALIS?

Changing schedules

Different shifts can make it difficult to keep a consistent sleep schedule.

Fatigue

Not getting enough sleep can affect alertness, concentration, memory, mood, and performance.

Mission operations

Sleep opportunities need to work around important mission events and crew responsibilities.

03 • Our Solution

How VITALIS works

Sleep Planner

Creates recommendations using sleep history, fatigue, hours awake, caffeine, shifts, and mission events.

Fatigue Score

Uses a 0–100 score to estimate fatigue and guide recommendations.

Shift Scheduling

Supports Night, Morning, and Evening shifts.

Check-In

Crew can record sleep, alertness, fatigue, and recommendation effectiveness.

Food & Caffeine

Tracks information that can be considered by the sleep planner.

Feedback

Saved feedback can help VITALIS adjust future recommendations.

09 • Testing

What we learned

Early testing

Five test subjects were used during the first prototype testing.

Problem found

The first version gave everyone a 22:00 sleep time, showing that it was not personalized enough.

Our response

We added more inputs so schedules could respond to individual sleep, fatigue, shifts, caffeine, and mission information.
