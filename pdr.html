Overview

What a PDR is

A Preliminary Design Review is an early checkpoint. The team presents its design and plan before the final build, and reviewers check that the approach can meet the requirements and point out risks while changes are still easy to make. Our review gave us comments from the judges, Ms. Westover and Mr. Hayes, and from the other teams.

What reviewers look for

Does the design solve the stated problem for the real users, and what could go wrong?

List every comment, group the repeats, and decide what goes into the app and in what order.

Why repeats matter

When both judges raise the same point, it moves to the top of our list.
